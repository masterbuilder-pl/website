This unique diorama was designed with the LEGO Star Wars 75359 – Battle Pack: 332nd Company Clone Troopers in mind, the elite unit led by Ahsoka Tano during the dramatic events of the Clone Wars.

<img src="/public/diorama75359/river-2.jpg" alt="diorama75359" class="post-image" />

The scene depicts a fragment of a planet with a water element – a realistic terrain built from sandy bricks, enriched with rock formations, lush vegetation, and a clear stream.
### The photos show classic Phase I clones, due to the unavailability of 332nd Legion soldiers at Bricklink Studio.”

<img src="/public/diorama75359/river-1.jpg" alt="diorama75359" class="post-image" />

The diorama beautifully showcases both the minifigures from the battle pack and the build included in the set, transforming a simple set into a striking collector’s display. It’s a perfect choice for both LEGO enthusiasts and Star Wars fans who want to present their set in a unique way.

<img src="/public/diorama75359/river-3.jpg" alt="diorama75359" class="post-image" />

The instructions for the vehicle can be downloaded for free from the [LEGO](https://www.lego.com/pl-pl/service/building-instructions/75359) website.
