I’m continuing my series of mini LEGO dioramas featuring collectible minifigures from Series 28.
This is the second model in the cycle, where I create small scenes that match each minifigure’s personality.

<img src="/public/frogdiorama/frog1.jpg" alt="frog" class="post-image" />

This time, we’re venturing into a swamp — damp, green, and full of vegetation. The frog-costumed minifigure feels right at home here, relaxing among moss, mud, and dense swamp plants. A tall pine and tiny details complete the natural, peaceful vibe of the scene.

This is just the beginning — each upcoming diorama will showcase a new minifigure in its unique environment.
