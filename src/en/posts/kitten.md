I’m continuing my series of LEGO mini dioramas featuring collectible minifigures from Series 28.
This is another build in the cycle where I create small scenes tailored to the personality of each minifigure.

This time, the spotlight is on the Cat Costume minifigure 🐱
An urban alleyway, a brick wall, and a trash can form its natural habitat — a place of nighttime wanderings, quiet encounters, and hunting for… something to eat. Surrounded by other cats and holding a ball of yarn, the minifigure feels right at home. Subtle details and a muted color palette help create a calm, slightly street-style atmosphere for the scene.

This is just the beginning — each upcoming diorama will feature a new minifigure and its unique environment.
Let me know which minifigure you’d like to see next! 🧱✨
