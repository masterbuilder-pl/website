At Klockon 2025 in Będzin I adopted a tiny piggy. 🐖 When I got back home, I thought: “This little fellow needs friends and a place to live!” So I built a mini scene with two more pigs and a herdsman to keep them company. It’s not a huge build, but it gives off cozy forest vibes and feels like a slice of simple countryside life. 🐷
