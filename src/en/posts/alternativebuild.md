I present to you my first alternate model. I built it using elements from set 72688, as well as a few additional parts ([2420](https://www.lego.com/pl-pl/pick-and-build/pick-a-brick?icmp=PAB_All_Pieces&query=2420),
 [91988](https://www.lego.com/pl-pl/pick-and-build/pick-a-brick?icmp=PAB_All_Pieces&query=91988),
 [18646](https://www.lego.com/pl-pl/pick-and-build/pick-a-brick?icmp=PAB_All_Pieces&query=18646),
 [3023](https://www.lego.com/pl-pl/pick-and-build/pick-a-brick?icmp=PAB_All_Pieces&query=3023)). It was a completely new adventure for me — from the initial idea, through multiple attempts, all the way to the moment when the build finally started to look like something that could actually exist.

The construction took me quite some time — I rebuilt the walls many times, adjusted the proportions, and looked for ways to make the whole thing both stable and visually interesting. In the end, I managed to create a model that not only holds together but also stands out with its own character.

I hope that my first alternate project will appeal to you and perhaps inspire someone to try making their own modifications to LEGO sets!
