The seventh vignette in my series of small scenes featuring Collectible Minifigures (Series 28) 🧱✨
This time the Peacock takes the stage 🦚 – we’re already past the halfway point of the project!

<img src="/public/peacock/peacock2.jpg" alt="peacock" class="post-image" />

The scene shows a peaceful garden corner with a decorative fountain filled with turquoise water and topped with a small golden statue. Next to it stands the peacock in an intense blue outfit with its fan of feathers proudly displayed.

In the background, you can see a fragment of a wall and pink Japanese cherry blossom trees (sakura), giving the whole scene a calm, spring atmosphere.
