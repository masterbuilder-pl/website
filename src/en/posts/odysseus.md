Odysseus – The Odyssey BrickHeadz
This custom LEGO® BrickHeadz model depicts Odysseus, the legendary king of Ithaca and hero of Homer’s Odyssey.
The idea for this build was inspired by Christopher Nolan’s film The Odyssey. I really enjoyed the film and wanted to create my own LEGO version of Odysseus.

You can purchase instructions on [Rebrickable](https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-271346/masterbuilder2222/odysseus-brickheadz-from-the-odyssey/#details).
