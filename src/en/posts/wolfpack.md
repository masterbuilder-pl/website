Are you looking for a home for your Wolfpack faction miniatures? Or maybe a simple, small tower model with a free instruction guide and a minimal number of pieces? This model is perfect for you!

The Wolfpack Fortress on a 16×16 base 🐺🏔️ is a small yet very atmospheric structure set in a rugged mountain environment. The model depicts a tiny stronghold guarded by a Wolfpack faction member, keeping watch over the main entrance. The construction is designed to capture the feeling of a secluded, rocky hideout — with massive walls, simple defensive elements, and distinctive details associated with this group. 🧱⚔️

<img src="/public/wolfpack/wolfpack.jpg" alt="tower" class="post-image" />

Despite its limited space, the model manages to retain a clear sense of character: a small but sturdy fortress hidden in the mountains, giving the impression of an observation point or a shelter for a small squad. The rugged, rocky landscape emphasizes its isolation while naturally creating a background that invites further expansion — with additional terrain pieces, towers, camps, or larger castle complexes. 🏰⛰️

<img src="/public/wolfpack/wolfpack1.jpg" alt="tower" class="post-image" />

This model is designed for fans of medieval constructions as well as those who enjoy making the most out of a small format. Simple, clear, and very “wolfpacky” in its character. 🐺✨

<img src="/public/wolfpack/wolfpack2.jpg" alt="tower" class="post-image" />

Additionally, the fortress is designed to be part of something much larger. I plan to eventually build an entire Wolfpack mountain complex — with more fortresses, paths, and rocky formations. This construction would serve as a guard post, the first line of defense in this expanding wolf kingdom. 🏔️🛡️
