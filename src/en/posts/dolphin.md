The eighth vignette in my series of small scenes featuring Collectible Minifigures
This time the Dolphin takes the stage 🐬 — in a much darker, sci-fi atmosphere.

<img src="/public/dolphin/dolphin(2).jpeg" alt="dolpfhin" class="post-image" />

The scene presents a futuristic laboratory where an experiment is underway to create a dolphin designed to spy on marine animals. Sealed inside a cylindrical cryo chamber as “Subject A-13,” it remains in stasis, while the display indicates active cryo suspension and rising neural activity. The concept was inspired by Avatar — the fusion of nature and advanced technology, and the idea of interfering with the natural order.


At the top of the structure, I added a strange, raw statue — a symbolic “creator” or perhaps the overseer of the experiment. It adds a subtle sense of unease and suggests that something bigger is behind the project.

What do you think about this shift into science fiction?
